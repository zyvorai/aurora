# Partner Enablement

## Purpose

Partner Enablement — Aurora surface at `/products/:id/partner`.

## When to use it

- Operate **Partner Enablement** when your job matches this page
- Admin lands on Full Forge by default; editors on Sales; approvers/viewers on Brief
- Confirm auth and that workers are up if ingest never completes

## How to get there

- Route: `/products/:id/partner`
- Nav: **Product → Partner Enablement**

## Notes

- Replace `:id` with your product id from `/dashboard`.

## Operate from the console (UX)

1. Product → Partner.
2. Read product profile.
3. Co-branded outreach → Forge outreach.
4. **Empty / fail:** —.
5. **Success:** Profile readable; template opens.

Use `http://<host>:3000` for the web UI and `http://<host>:8000/health` for the API. Never publish lab IPs in customer docs.
