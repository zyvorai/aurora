Aurora

Turn your product into an AI salesperson.

Point it at your website or docs. It builds a grounded knowledge base, then runs marketing, sales and solution agents - with a human approving everything that goes out.

Try it in five minutesRead the docs

From zero to a working demo

Four containers, one command, your data stays on your machine.

  1. Install

    One line on macOS, Linux or Windows. Or a Helm chart for Kubernetes.

  2. Add your product

    Paste a website or docs URL. Aurora ingests it and builds a product profile.

  3. Review what it makes

    SEO audits, content, outreach. You approve; then it publishes.

What you can test

Everything below works in the PoC. Where something needs your credentials (publishing channels, keyword data), it says so.

Grounded answers

Claims are checked against your own sources and cited. No invented pitches.

SEO scoring

Audit any public page and score drafts. Keyword data is labelled measured or estimated.

Human-approved publishing

Approval is tied to the exact content that goes out. Edit it and it needs approval again.

Background agents

Research, outreach and proposals run on the server while you do something else.

Your LLM

Bring an OpenAI-compatible key, or run Ollama locally (GPU on Apple Silicon).

Native Mac app

A SwiftUI client that connects to any Aurora server. Coming soon.
